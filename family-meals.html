Frequently Asked

How many people does a Family Pack biryani feed?

A Family Pack serves 3–4 people. For larger groups, our Jumbo Pack serves 5–6 people. Both come in Chicken, Goat, and Veggie Dum Biryani.

Can I mix biryani flavors in a Family or Jumbo Pack order?

Call us at (469) 960-3300 and we'll help you build an order that mixes Chicken, Goat and Veggie Dum Biryani to fit your group.

Can I get a Family Pack or Jumbo Pack delivered?

Order online for guaranteed pickup at our Plano kitchen, or check DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub for delivery availability to your address.

Is Family Pack and Jumbo Pack biryani 100% Zabihah Halal?

Yes — every Family Pack and Jumbo Pack biryani is 100% Zabihah Halal, Hafsah Certified and HMS Certified, made fresh at our Plano kitchen.
