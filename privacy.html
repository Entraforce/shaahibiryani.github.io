Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shaahi Biryani ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile app and website (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Order information — items you order, order history, pickup or delivery selection, and any delivery address and special instructions you provide.
  • Payment information — card payments are processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store or have access to your full card number; Stripe handles card data directly and securely.
  • Loyalty & rewards — points you earn and redeem, and your rewards tier.
  • Device & notification data — if you enable notifications, a device token used to send you order-status updates.
  • Usage information — basic technical data (such as app version and general activity) needed to operate and improve the Services.

Ask Shaahi — AI Dining Concierge

Our website and mobile app include Ask Shaahi, an AI-powered dining concierge that helps you explore the menu, ask questions, and get recommendations.

When you choose to use Ask Shaahi, the messages you send and the relevant conversation context are sent to our AI service provider, Anthropic, so a reply can be generated. Alongside your message we send only what the conversation needs: the earlier messages in that conversation, whether you are ordering pickup or dine-in, your party size, whether you are using the app or the website, and which part of the menu you are looking at.

Our implementation is designed not to send your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, payment information, account ID, order history, or precise location as part of an ordinary Ask Shaahi conversation. Please do not type sensitive personal information or payment details into Ask Shaahi.

We do not store the text of Ask Shaahi conversations in our restaurant database. We keep limited operational information — such as technical request data, the length of a message, which service it came from, and pseudonymised identifiers — to keep the feature reliable and to prevent abuse.

Anthropic handles this information under its own commercial API terms. Anthropic's published terms state that data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default, and that API inputs and outputs are automatically deleted within 30 days, subject to limited exceptions such as where longer retention is required by law or to enforce its usage policy. See Anthropic's Privacy Center.

Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

Our website uses cookies and similar browser storage. Some are needed for the site to work — for example, remembering what is in your cart or that you have already made a cookie choice. These are always active.

With your permission, we also use:

  • Google Analytics — to measure how the site is used, so we can improve it.
  • Google Ads — to measure whether our advertising works and, where enabled, to show you our ads on other sites (remarketing). This may involve Google advertising domains such as doubleclick.net.

These technologies place cookies or similar identifiers in your browser (for example _ga, _ga_<id> and _gcl_au) that let Google recognise a browser across visits and measure site usage and advertising effectiveness.

We do not load Google Analytics or Google Ads until you accept. On your first visit you are asked to choose, and nothing is sent to Google before you do. If you decline, these tags are not loaded at all. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the site footer.

Ask Shaahi, the menu, your cart, reservations and ordering all work normally whether you accept or decline — your choice here only affects analytics and advertising.

In our mobile app we do not use advertising cookies or Google advertising tags. The app uses PostHog for product analytics — which screens are opened and which features are used — to help us improve it. The text of your Ask Shaahi messages is never sent to our analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To take, prepare, and fulfill your food orders (pickup and delivery).
  • To process payments and send receipts.
  • To run our loyalty/rewards program.
  • To send order-status updates and respond to your requests.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Services.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with the service providers who help us run the Services, and only as needed to provide them:

  • Stripe — payment processing. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — secure hosting of your account and order data.
  • Apple / Google / Expo — delivery of push notifications, if you enable them.
  • Anthropic — processing your Ask Shaahi messages to generate a reply.
  • PostHog — product analytics in our mobile app: which screens are opened and which features are used, so we can improve the app. It does not receive the text of your Ask Shaahi messages.
  • Google (Analytics & Ads) — website analytics and advertising measurement, only if you accept analytics and advertising cookies.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or property of our customers or business.

Data Retention & Deleting Your Account

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

You can delete your account at any time directly in the app: open Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account and associated personal profile data. Some order and payment records may be retained where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

To request deletion or ask any privacy question, contact us using the details below and we will respond.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Access or update your account information in the app.
  • Turn notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Change your analytics and advertising cookie choice at any time using Cookie Settings in the website footer.
  • Delete your account as described above.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your data; contact us to exercise them.

Changes to This Policy
