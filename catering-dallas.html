Halal Hyderabadi Catering in Dallas

From Uptown ballrooms to North Dallas estates — we bring royal biryani, kababs and Hyderabadi spreads straight from our Plano kitchen to your Dallas event.

Just south of our Plano kitchen via US-75, Shaahi Biryani brings authentic Hyderabadi, Indian and Pakistani halal cuisine to catering across Dallas. We regularly serve weddings, walimas, engagements and corporate events at venues including the Hilton Anatole, the Westin Galleria Dallas and the Rosewood Mansion on Turtle Creek, and homes throughout Uptown, Preston Hollow and Highland Park. Every meat dish is 100% Zabihah Halal, Hafsah Certified and HMS Certified — built on a Hyderabadi tradition our family has served since 2017.
