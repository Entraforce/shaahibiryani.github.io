Our Legacy

Our Story

1 / 3
Chapter One

Born from a Royal Kitchen

Shaahi Biryani was born from a deep love for the rich culinary heritage of Hyderabad — a city where biryani is an art form passed through generations of royal kitchens.

Every grain of saffron-kissed basmati carries the memory of centuries-old Nawabi recipes that once graced the tables of royalty.

✦ ── ✦ ── ✦
2 / 3
Chapter Two

Crafted with Devotion

We source only the finest long-grain basmati, grind our spices fresh each morning, and follow traditional dum-cooking methods perfected over centuries.

Our chefs rise before dawn to prepare the day's akhni — the aromatic broth that forms the soul of every biryani we serve. No shortcuts. No compromise.
