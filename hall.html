Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can Shaahi Biryani's private hall in Plano accommodate?

Up to 132 guests, in an elegantly appointed private dining space at 3421 E Renner Rd, Suite 110, Plano, TX 75074 — the same building as the restaurant.

Is catering included when I book the private event hall?

Yes — every hall booking includes full Hyderabadi halal catering, from biryani trays to multi-course spreads, prepared fresh and 100% Zabihah Halal, Hafsah Certified and HMS Certified.

What types of events can I host at Shaahi Biryani's hall?

Weddings, walimas, engagements, birthdays, Eid and festive gatherings, and corporate events — with menus customized to your occasion and guest count.

How do I book the private hall?

Fill out the enquiry form above with your event date, guest count and event type, or call (469) 960-3300 — our team typically responds within 24 hours.

Is the private hall 100% Zabihah Halal?

Yes — the same certifications that cover Shaahi Biryani's kitchen apply to every event catered in the hall: 100% Zabihah Halal, Hafsah Certified and HMS Certified.
