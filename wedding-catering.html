Frequently Asked

Does Shaahi Biryani cater weddings and walimas across DFW?

Yes — Shaahi Biryani caters weddings, walimas, engagements and receptions across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, either at our own private hall in Plano (up to 132 guests) or at a venue of your choice, for events of 50 to 3,000+ guests.

Should I book Shaahi Biryani's own hall or have you cater at my venue?

Both are available. Our private hall in Plano seats up to 132 guests and includes the space itself — ideal for smaller weddings, walimas and receptions. For larger events, or if you already have a venue booked, we cater on-site anywhere across DFW with full-service live stations and tray service.

What's included in Shaahi Biryani's wedding catering menu?

Custom Hyderabadi menus built around Goat and Chicken Dum Biryani, Haleem, Butter Chicken, Chicken Tikka Masala, Sheek and Chapli Kababs, tandoori grills, Apollo Fish, fresh naan and Hyderabadi desserts — available in Half Pan and Full Pan sizes, with vegetarian and Jain options on request.

Is Shaahi Biryani's wedding catering 100% Zabihah Halal?

Yes — every dish served at your wedding or walima is 100% Zabihah Halal, Hafsah Certified and HMS Certified, prepared fresh at our Plano kitchen the morning of your event.

How far in advance should I book wedding catering?

We recommend booking as early as possible for weddings, especially during peak wedding season, since large events require menu planning and guest-count confirmation. Call (469) 960-3300 or submit the quote form above and we'll confirm availability and next steps.

How many guests can Shaahi Biryani's wedding catering serve?

From intimate gatherings of 50 to large walimas and receptions of 3,000+ guests — our catering scales to your event size, whether at our Plano hall or at your chosen venue anywhere across DFW.
