Frequently Asked Questions

Which venues has Shaahi Biryani catered at in DFW?

Shaahi Biryani has catered at 11+ venues across DFW, including Shalimar Banquet Hall, Verona Villa Banquet Hall, Serene Banquet, Crystal Banquet, Richardson Women's Club, Holiday Inn Fort Worth, Flourish Events Dallas, a Hilton Hotel, Golden Eagle Ranch, The Spring Events, and Convention Plaza in Irving — serving 3,000+ guests across 50+ events.

Can Shaahi Biryani cater at my own venue or event space?

Yes — Shaahi Biryani caters at venues throughout the DFW metroplex, not just our own hall. Call (469) 960-3300 or request a quote to confirm availability at your specific venue.

Does Shaahi Biryani have its own event space too?

Yes — in addition to catering at outside venues, Shaahi Biryani has a private event hall in Plano accommodating up to 132 guests, available to book directly.
