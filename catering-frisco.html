Halal Hyderabadi Catering in Frisco

From Stonebriar weddings to corporate dinners near The Star — we bring royal biryani, kababs and Hyderabadi spreads straight from our Plano kitchen to your Frisco event.

Just minutes south of Frisco, Shaahi Biryani has been catering authentic Hyderabadi, Indian and Pakistani halal cuisine across the DFW metroplex since 2017. We routinely serve weddings, walimas, engagements and corporate events at major Frisco venues — including the Embassy Suites by Hilton Frisco, the Westin Stonebriar, Hilton Frisco, the Frisco Conference Center and family residences across Stonebriar, Phillips Creek Ranch and Starwood. Every dish is 100% Zabihah Halal, Hafsah Certified and HMS Certified — and made fresh the morning of your event.
