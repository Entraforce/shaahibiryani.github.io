# Phase 14 — Competitor Analysis

Date: 2026-10-30
Branch: `seo/phase14-competitors` (off `main`)
Scope: a deeper technical/content comparison against 4 real local
competitors already surfaced via live search in Phases 5 and 7 — this
phase visits their actual websites rather than re-searching for names.

**Method**: direct fetches of competitor sites, compared against
Shaahi's already-audited architecture (Phases 1, 2, 5). **No keyword
rankings, traffic estimates, or backlink data are reported anywhere in
this document** — no commercial SEO tool access exists this session,
and the master prompt explicitly prohibits fabricating these. Where a
competitor's site couldn't be found or fetched, that's stated rather
than guessed around (Aslam Biryani House's official site couldn't be
located via search; substituted with Biryaniify, which fetched
successfully).

---

## Executive summary

1. **Halal certification is a wide-open competitive gap.** None of
   the 4 competitors checked — Bawarchi Biryanis, Biryaniify, Just
   Punjabi Foods, Dil Se Desi — mention halal or Zabihah certification
   anywhere on their sites. Shaahi leads with "100% Zabihah Halal,
   Hafsah + HMS Certified" on every single page. For any halal-specific
   search, Shaahi has effectively no real competition among these 4 on
   that axis.
2. **Zero visible structured data on any of the 4 competitor sites
   checked** — no `schema.org`/JSON-LD markup detected on any of them,
   versus Shaahi's comprehensive Restaurant/FAQPage/Service/
   BreadcrumbList schema sitewide (Phase 2). A real, confirmed
   structural advantage.
3. **No competitor has anything resembling Shaahi's 36-page city
   network.** Each competitor is a single generic site; their
   geographic reach is whatever that one site happens to rank for.
   Shaahi has dedicated, differentiated pages for 19 DFW cities on both
   the ordering and catering side.
4. **Shaahi's stated catering capacity (50 to 3,000+ guests) is more
   specific and prominent** than any of the 4 competitors' catering
   pages, none of which state a clear guest-count range.
5. **Two things worth knowing about, not necessarily worth copying**:
   Bawarchi Biryanis emphasizes live cooking-station theatrics (dosa
   counter, chaat, jalebi prepared on-site) as a vivid in-person
   differentiator that Shaahi's text-forward catering pages don't
   visually emphasize the same way; Bawarchi also has native iOS/
   Android apps, which is a bigger investment than likely makes sense
   for a single-location restaurant — flagged as an observation, not a
   recommendation.

## What this means for Shaahi

The site's existing technical investment — structured data (Phase 2),
the city-page network (Phase 5), and the halal-certification messaging
already baked into every page — isn't just good practice, it's a real,
confirmed gap none of these 4 real competitors have closed. This is
worth knowing as context for prioritization in Phase 15: the
foundational work already done here is a genuine moat, not just SEO
hygiene for its own sake.

The one area worth a look (not a commitment) is whether Shaahi's
catering pages could visually emphasize the live-station / event-day
experience the way Bawarchi's does — currently Shaahi's catering pages
are text-and-FAQ-forward. Not proposing a specific change here; noting
it as a real pattern seen in a competitor that converts (minimum 50
guests, same category) as something to be aware of, not something to
copy wholesale.
